# References

## In plain words

Every source cited in the documentation and in the class, in full. The entries follow the Brazilian ABNT standard
(NBR 6023) and match the reference pages of the class deck one to one, so a reader can find each of them there. The
two sources of the cost computation (Anthropic's pricing page and the Banco Central PTAX rate) complete the bases of
the method. The groups are those of the deck: where each claim came from, the bases of the architecture, the bases of
the method, and the music of the videos.
